# Way More Fish

**Way More Fish** is an open Louisiana coastal fishing-conditions and community-data project designed to help anglers make better-informed decisions about **where and when to fish**.

The project brings together practical environmental information and local knowledge in one place so anglers can compare conditions across fishing locations.

## Project goals

Way More Fish is intended to combine useful factors such as:

- tides and tidal movement
- observed and forecast wind
- weather conditions
- barometric pressure
- water conditions and clarity
- satellite and remotely sensed observations when available
- verified fishing locations and coordinates
- community fishing reports and local knowledge
- condition-based scoring or ranking for comparing fishing spots

The goal is not to guarantee fish. It is to organize the best available information so anglers can make their own decisions.

## Geographic focus

The initial focus is coastal southwest Louisiana, including areas such as:

- Calcasieu Lake / Big Lake
- West Cove
- Cameron Parish
- Rutherford Beach
- nearby Louisiana coastal waters

Additional Louisiana locations may be added as the project grows.

## Data quality

Way More Fish is intended to be a **credible and transparent application**.

Fishing locations should be checked against reliable geographic sources before being added. Environmental observations and forecasts should identify their source whenever practical.

Community observations can be valuable, but they should be distinguished from official observations, forecasts, and measured data.

## Safety and responsibility

Way More Fish provides informational fishing-condition data only.

Conditions on the water can change quickly. Users are responsible for checking official weather and marine forecasts, navigation information, closures, fishing regulations, and safety conditions before heading out.
